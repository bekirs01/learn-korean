Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary is an offline app for beginners. Study Hangul, Korean words, phrases, and quizzes on iPhone and iPad. Optionally send a short study list to Apple Watch.

No account is required. Learning content and your progress stay on your devices.

Contact

This page is the App Store support destination for Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary. Use the troubleshooting steps below first.

No public email address is published for this app. For an issue this page does not cover, open a GitHub Issue at github.com/bekirs01/learn-korean/issues. Issues on that repository are public, so do not include personal information you do not want visible. After the App Store listing is live, you can also use the Support link on the product page, which points here.

Supported devices

  • iPhone and iPad (iOS 13 or later), including landscape on iPad
  • Apple Watch companion app (watchOS 10 or later), installed from the Watch app on iPhone after the iPhone app is installed
  • The Watch app is not a standalone App Store product. It needs the iPhone app for the first learning-plan send
  • Internet is not required for Hangul, words, phrases, quizzes, or pronunciation once the app is installed

Pronunciation does not play

  • Turn the device volume up and disable Silent Mode if you expect speaker output.
  • Confirm Settings → Sound effects is on for tap and quiz sounds. Korean pronunciation uses a separate play control on learning cards.
  • Korean text-to-speech needs a Korean voice installed on the device. On iPhone, open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and download a Korean (South Korea) voice if none is present. Yuna is the preferred voice when available.
  • On Apple Watch, install a Korean watchOS voice if speech stays silent. That is expected when no Korean voice is installed.
  • The app does not use the microphone and does not record your voice.

Hangul, quizzes, favorites, and progress

  • Hangul letters come from the bundled alphabet. Marking an item Learned applies to words and phrases, not to a separate Hangul cloud profile.
  • Quiz scores stay on the results screen for that session. Missed items are stored on the device as content identifiers so you can review them later.
  • Favorites and learned flags are stored only on the device. They are not synced to a developer account.

Apple Watch is empty

  • Install Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary on iPhone, then install the Learn Korean Watch app from the Watch app on iPhone.
  • On iPhone, open Learn Korean → Watch, choose words or phrases, and send the plan. The Watch shows that list; it does not download a catalog from the internet.
  • Keep Watch and iPhone paired. A saved plan can remain on the Watch when the iPhone is not reachable, but the first send needs the Watch app installed.
  • This version does not send remote push notifications. Study-window settings on iPhone are local; they are not a cloud reminder service.

Wrong translation language

Open Settings and set Translation language. That changes learner glosses and interface language. Korean Hangul on cards does not change.

Progress, favorites, or quiz misses disappeared

Those items are stored only on the device. Deleting the app, clearing device storage, or setting up a new device without a full device backup removes them. Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary does not sync progress to a developer cloud. Apple device backups you control (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may restore local state; that is Apple’s backup, not an app account.

Notifications and accounts

Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary does not create user accounts. There is nothing to sign in to and no account-deletion flow. This version does not send remote push notifications from a developer server. To delete local data, remove the iPhone or iPad app and, if installed, the Watch app.
