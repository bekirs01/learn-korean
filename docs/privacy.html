Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Legal hub Support

This Privacy Policy describes how Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary (home-screen name: Learn Korean) handles information. It applies to the iPhone and iPad app (com.bekirsucikaran.learnkorean) and the Apple Watch app (com.bekirsucikaran.learnkorean.watchkitapp).

The operator of the app is the independent developer of Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary. This policy is written from a review of the current application source code. It does not describe services the app does not include.

App Store Connect privacy label: Data Not Collected. Under Apple’s definition, the developer does not transmit personal data off the device in a way that the developer or advertising partners can retain.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary does not operate a backend, user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other developer-accessible servers. The developer cannot see your Hangul or vocabulary progress, favorites, quiz history, Watch plan, or Settings.

Nothing in the app is transmitted to the developer or to a third-party data broker for advertising or tracking.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect or request:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other contact information
  • Account credentials or sign-in data
  • Device advertising identifiers or tracking signals
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Health, fitness, financial, or government ID data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or speech-recognition recordings
  • Browsing history, search history, or user-generated social content
  • Purchase records beyond whatever Apple may process if you buy the app through the App Store

Information stored only on your devices

Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary is offline-first. Learning content (Hangul letters, Korean words and phrases, translations, images, and sound effects) is bundled in the app. The following items can be stored locally on your iPhone, iPad, and, if you use it, Apple Watch:

  • Translation language preference, plus sound and haptics settings
  • Favorite word and phrase identifiers
  • Learned word and phrase identifiers (vocabulary progress you mark in the app)
  • Hangul and vocabulary quiz information: miss records store a content identifier, miss count, last quiz type, and a timestamp. The app does not keep a cloud gradebook, typed answers, session scores, or achievements
  • Apple Watch learning-plan draft (selected items, local study-window settings, and last local sync status)

Hangul study itself uses the bundled alphabet. There is no separate Hangul “learned” cloud profile. Alphabet items can appear in the on-device quiz-miss list if you answer them incorrectly.

On Apple Watch, a local cache can keep the latest synced Korean text, your translation, and learner-language code so cards remain available when the iPhone is briefly unreachable.

This on-device data is not an account. Uninstalling the iPhone app, deleting the Watch app, or erasing the device removes it. There is no cloud backup provided by Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary.

Apple Watch

If you pair an Apple Watch and send a learning plan, the iPhone uses Apple WatchConnectivity to copy selected study items to your Watch. That transfer stays between your paired devices. It is not uploaded to the developer. The Watch app does not use a microphone, does not run speech recognition, and does not send push notifications through a developer server. Study-window times stored in the plan are local scheduling data, not a cloud reminder service.

Speech and audio

Korean pronunciation uses bundled recordings when present, otherwise the device’s built-in text-to-speech (on iOS, Apple’s system speech synthesizer) with a Korean ko-KR voice when one is installed. The app does not record your voice, does not use the microphone, and does not send spoken text to a developer speech API. Sound effects are bundled audio files played on the device.

Third-party software

The app is built with Flutter and uses on-device libraries for local preferences, local audio playback, and native text-to-speech. Those libraries run on your device. This version does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, authentication providers, or in-app purchase SDKs.

Apple provides the App Store, device operating system, WatchConnectivity, and system speech. Apple’s handling of App Store data is described in Apple’s own privacy information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary does not include analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking. It does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework.

Purchases

This version of the app does not implement in-app purchases or subscriptions. If you obtain the app from the App Store, payment is processed by Apple, not by a Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary server.

Permissions

The iOS app does not declare microphone, camera, speech-recognition, location, tracking, or photo-library usage descriptions, because those capabilities are not used. Android debug and profile builds may request network access for Flutter tooling only; the product’s learning features do not require the internet.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect it. On-device data is not sent to our servers.

Apple device backups

On-device preferences and learning state may be included in an encrypted iPhone, iPad, or Watch backup that you control if you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup. That is an Apple system feature, not a Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary cloud. The developer cannot open, download, or restore those backups.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences, Hangul and vocabulary quiz-miss records, favorites, learned-item identifiers, and Watch plans remain on the device until you change them, clear app data, uninstall the app, delete the Watch app, or erase the device. The developer retains no user learning database and has no copy to export or erase on request.

To delete the on-device data: remove Learn Korean from iPhone or iPad, remove the Watch app from Apple Watch if installed, and optionally erase the device. Creating a new device without restoring a backup also starts with empty local state.

Security

Because learning state never leaves your devices through this app, the main safeguard is keeping the device locked and up to date. We do not operate a user data store that could be breached on our side.

Children’s privacy

Learn Korean Hangul Vocabulary is a general-audience education app. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly obtain personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use children’s data for advertising or tracking.

International users, GDPR, and California rights

The app can be used without creating a profile with us. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, the practical result is the same: we do not hold personal information about you that we could access, sell, or share. You can delete on-device data by removing the app. There is no developer-side copy to export or erase.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California privacy law.

Changes

If the app’s behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of a later version of the app after an update means the revised policy applies to that version.

Contact and support

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Support page. That page is the App Store support destination for this app.

There is no in-app email form and no developer mailbox is published here. For a written request, open a GitHub Issue on the public legal site repository: github.com/bekirs01/learn-korean/issues. Issues are public. Do not include personal information you do not want visible. You may also use the Support link on the App Store product page after the listing is live.
